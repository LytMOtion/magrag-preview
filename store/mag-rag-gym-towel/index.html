How the magnet works

A non-corrosive neodymium magnet with ~20 lb pull force is integrated into the towel edge — no hard corners, no added bulk. Press it against any steel surface — rack upright, bench frame, plate-loaded machine — and it holds until you pull it off. It will not hold on aluminum or non-metal surfaces.

Product details

  • 16" × 36" — sized to cover a standard gym bench
  • Premium microfiber — absorbent, soft, and quick drying. (Exact composition %: EDIT — confirm with supplier.)
  • Non-corrosive neodymium magnet — ~20 lb pull force
  • Six designs, one price

Care instructions

  • Machine wash cold with like colors
  • Hang dry or tumble dry low — avoid sustained high heat near the magnet
  • No bleach, no fabric softener (reduces microfiber absorbency)

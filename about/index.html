The story

It started on a dirty gym floor

Every session, the same ritual: drape the towel over the bench, watch it slide off mid-set, pick it up off a floor a thousand shoes have crossed, and pretend that's fine. It isn't fine.

MAGRAG came out of that frustration. After months of prototyping materials and magnet configurations, the answer turned out to be simple: a premium microfiber towel with a neodymium magnet built into its edge. Snap it onto the equipment you’re already using and it stays within reach — through the whole workout.

We’re a small company with one product and one standard: it has to solve the problem better than anything else you could bring to the gym. No gimmicks, no unnecessary complexity — thoughtful design that earns its place in your bag.

What we hold ourselves to

Three principles

01

Strong magnets

A non-corrosive neodymium magnet with roughly 20 lb of pull, integrated into the towel edge. It either holds or the product doesn't ship.

02

Premium quality

Premium microfiber chosen for absorbency, softness, and quick drying. Every towel is inspected before it goes out.

03

Built for athletes

Sized for a real bench, tested on real racks, designed around how people actually train — not how towels are usually sold.
